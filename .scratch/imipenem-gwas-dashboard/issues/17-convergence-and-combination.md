# 17: Convergence and combination analysis

**What to build:** The two stages that make a hit interpretable. Public archives over-represent clinical and outbreak isolates and a few high-risk clones, so without these a scan confidently reports clonal structure as a resistance mechanism.

**Blocked by:** 16

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A determinant is called convergent only when it appears in at least two independent lineages.
- [ ] Widespread determinants are distinguished from rare ones.
- [ ] Co-occurrence is tested across the determinant classes, corrected for multiple testing, and respects a minimum cell count.
- [ ] No stage states or implies that detecting a determinant establishes phenotypic resistance.
- [ ] Candidate calls are not promoted to confirmed, per configuration.
